

# Bank Loan Risk Analysis

## Objective
Explore how borrower characteristics relate to loan defaults.

## Dataset
10 fictional loan records created for educational purposes.

## Questions
- What percentage of borrowers defaulted?
- How do credit scores differ across groups?
- How does loan size compare with annual income?

## Findings
- 40% of borrowers defaulted
- Credit scores are about 130 pts different between 715 for borrowers who didn't default and 585 for borrowers who DID default
- the lower the annual income, the higher the loan size

## Limitations
This synthetic dataset is too small to support
general conclusions or reliable predictions.

## Next Steps
